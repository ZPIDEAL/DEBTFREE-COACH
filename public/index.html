Questions

What exactly does DebtFree Coach do?

Three things. First, it's a debt payoff calculator that runs your real numbers — every balance, APR, and minimum — and gives you an exact payoff order with your debt-free date, month by month. Second, a 24/7 coach chat that answers questions using your numbers. Third, an investing academy that teaches the basics for when you're debt-free. One subscription, $9.99/month after a 7-day free trial.

How does it calculate my debt-free date?

You enter each debt's balance, APR, and minimum payment, plus what extra you can pay monthly. The payoff engine then simulates two proven strategies — avalanche (highest APR first, least interest) and snowball (smallest balance first, fastest wins) — and shows you the exact month you'd be debt-free under each, plus total interest. Move the what-if slider to see how extra payments change the date in real time.

Debt snowball vs. avalanche — which should I pick?

Avalanche costs less in interest because it kills the highest-APR debt first. Snowball frees up cash flow faster because it kills the smallest balance first, which keeps some people motivated. The app shows you both side by side on your numbers so you can see the actual dollar difference — then you choose.

What kinds of debt can I track?

Anything with a balance and an interest rate: credit cards, personal loans, auto loans, student loans, medical debt. Add as many as you have.

Is this financial advice?

No. DebtFree Coach is financial education. It does the math and teaches the concepts — it never tells you what to buy, and it names no stocks, funds, or tickers. For personalized advice, talk to a licensed fiduciary. Investing involves risk, including loss of principal.
