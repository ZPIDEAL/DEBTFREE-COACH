No-Refund Policy

Policy version: 2026-10-15-draft1 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

The short version

All charges are final and non-refundable once posted. The free trial exists so you can evaluate the service at no cost before any charge happens.

How billing works

  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. A card is collected at signup, but the first $9.99 charge happens on day 7.
  • Cancel anytime during the trial and you pay $0 — your card is never charged.
  • After the trial, the subscription is $9.99/month, billed monthly until you cancel.

After a charge posts

Once a monthly charge has posted to your card, it is not refundable — including if you cancel mid-period, stop using the app, or forgot to cancel. Cancelling stops the next charge; you keep full access until the end of the billing period you already paid for.

How to cancel

Cancel in two taps from inside the app: Account (👤) → Manage subscription / cancel. No phone calls, no retention maze. You can also email QuestPublications901@gmail.com from your account email if you need help cancelling.

Billing mistakes

If you believe you were charged in error (for example, a charge posted after you cancelled during the trial), email QuestPublications901@gmail.com with your account email and the charge date. We will investigate — genuine billing errors will be corrected.
