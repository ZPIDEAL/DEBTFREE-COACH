Privacy Policy

Policy version: 2026-10-15-draft1 · Last updated: October 15, 2026

1. What we collect

  • Account info: your email address (at signup) and your name (during onboarding).
  • Financial inputs you enter: debt balances, APRs, minimum payments, income, expenses, and savings — the numbers you type in to build your plan.
  • Usage: basic records needed to run the service (e.g. login sessions, payments you log).

We do not collect your phone number. We do not pull data from your bank — everything financial is entered by you manually.

2. How we use it

Your data is used only to provide and improve the service: running your payoff calculations, personalizing your coach and academy, managing your subscription, and communicating about your account. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data.
  • We never share your debt numbers or personal details with third parties for marketing.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. When you subscribe, your card details go directly to Stripe. We receive only what Stripe tells us about your subscription status (e.g. active, trialing, canceled) — we never see or store your full card number.

5. Cookies & sessions

We use a single httpOnly session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

6. Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes (never in plain text). Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your choices

  • Start over: you can wipe your plan data (debts, payments, profile answers) from the app's "Start over" option at any time; your account and subscription remain.
  • Delete your data: email QuestPublications901@gmail.com from your account email to request deletion of your account and data.
  • Demo mode: the demo stores nothing at all.

8. Children

DebtFree Coach is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here with a new version date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: QuestPublications901@gmail.com.
